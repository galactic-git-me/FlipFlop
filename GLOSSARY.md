# FlipFlop

FlipFlop matches customer workloads to a Playbook capability profile and a commercially viable PC.

## Language

**Playbook**:
The shared product framework relating customer use cases to capability tiers, workload requirements and suitable PC offers.
_Avoid_: Price list, budget ladder

**Playbook cell**:
A customer use case paired with a capability tier, defining a customer outcome and the criteria needed to substantiate it.
_Avoid_: Budget cell

**Capability tier**:
A level of useful capability within a use case, expressed through customer benefits and measurable workload requirements. A higher tier must justify a distinct benefit; not every use case needs three offers.
_Avoid_: Spending tier, price tier

**Customer budget**:
The customer's spending limit, used to filter commercially viable offers that satisfy their workload needs.
_Avoid_: Capability tier

**Selling price**:
The complete payable amount for a specific configuration and delivery offer, supported by current cost and market evidence.
_Avoid_: Capability level

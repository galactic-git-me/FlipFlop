# Project glossary

**Agent handoff event** — A validated, idempotent record that one backend worker publishes for another worker, with provenance references and a typed payload.

**Demand observation** — A dated source fact about interest, adoption, or buyer activity. Its measure and limitations stay attached; adoption share is not sales volume.

**Price range evidence** — A condition-, market-type-, identity-, and geography-specific summary of comparable delivered prices, with source provenance and sample counts.

**Delivery pace** — A Playbook sourcing promise class: Fast Track, Standard, or Flexible, each governed by supplier availability and delivery limits.

**Playbook decision** — An auditable choice to retain, replace, reprice, pause, or publish a compatible build cell under sourcing, margin, and movement policies.

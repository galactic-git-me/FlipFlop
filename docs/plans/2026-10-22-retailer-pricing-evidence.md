### Complete-system interpretation

The strongest direct entry gaming comparison is £899 for a 5600/5050/16GB/1TB system, while the lower AWD 5500/5050 headline requires configuration normalisation. An integrated-graphics 5600G system competes with a roughly £470 headline, not a dedicated-GPU system. The single £2,150 7800X3D/9060XT listing includes unusually long warranty/support and is insufficient to establish a market median. Sold-out configurable £1,880–£1,900 pages provide weak competitive context only. Specialist 9950X/5090 evidence is around £7,078 including VAT before unverified freight and requires a fresh quote.

Do not infer ideal prices or profit from these alone: match RAM, SSD, OS, GPU VRAM, warranty, condition, availability and total delivered VAT-inclusive price, then combine eligible comparisons with genuine sold records and complete landed component costs. No exact in-stock 9800X3D/non-XT 9070 or Ultra250K/non-XT9070 complete configured quote was established in this pass. A CyberPower 9800X3D/9070 page exposed configuration but no readable price; a Scan 9950X/5090 result returned 404. Both were excluded from numeric benchmarks.

## Customer proposition

“Choose your pace. Choose your price.” Explain that more sourcing time can produce a lower price for the same selected PC, while eligible Fast Track builds cost more for priority sourcing and workshop handling. Standard provides the comparison baseline. Do not claim uniqueness against competitors without research.

Display Fast Track, Standard and Flexible together at relevant storefront entry points, curated build detail, configuration and checkout. Each available mode shows the full payable total, estimated delivery date/range, price difference against Standard and a short explanation. The selected mode and total travel through cart, payment, confirmation and portal. Ready-to-Ship remains completed stock with its own fulfilment options.

Suggested wording, only when backed by a valid quote:

| Mode | Explanation |
|---|---|
| Fast Track | Need it sooner? Pay more for priority sourcing and building. |
| Standard | Our usual build and delivery timetable. |
| Flexible | Happy to wait? Give us more time to source your parts and save £X on this build. |

Do not invent £X, fixed delivery promises or a discount percentage. If Flexible cannot beat Standard while meeting profit and evidence gates, it must not be presented as a saving. Show it as unavailable as a savings offer, with a reason. Any equivalent substitution requires a separately disclosed and accepted configuration; used/refurbished parts require explicit condition consent. Waiting must not silently buy a different specification, condition or warranty.

## Playbook ownership and economics

Each customer-type × budget cell owns a versioned commercial policy linked to the existing canonical cell identity. Reconcile the current Playbook, curated segment and authored definition representations before adding another source of truth.

The policy references its core BOM and compatibility/capability constraints, eligible component upgrades, cases, software and paid services. It defines mode eligibility, minimum contribution and margin, allowable premiums and savings, promotion bounds, evidence freshness, and review thresholds. Store observations and immutable quotes in linked records rather than continually overwriting the authored Playbook or paid orders.

Calculate each mode from eligible supplier offers for the same specification. Include landed parts, supplier postage, labour, workshop priority cost, packaging, outbound courier, insurance, payment/channel fees, warranty and returns reserves, expected failures, overhead, and applicable tax treatment. Evaluate final contribution and margin after every promotion and upsell combination. Percentage-of-sale fees/reserves must be solved against the final selling price, not estimated from parts cost alone. Use Decimal money arithmetic and explicit rounding.

Compatibility facts must attach to the exact curated/custom catalogue listing selected by the Playbook. Capture explicitly labelled retailer properties with the listing URL and capture time, then present supported fields as review-only suggestions. Preserve technical properties from an exact identified product page even when its selected seller offer, price or stock is unavailable; keep those facts separate from last-good commercial evidence so they cannot make an offer purchasable. An operator must inspect the exact source before those fields enter `raw_specs.configurator` as reviewed evidence. Ignore ambiguous package dimensions, generated/AI measurements, and unlabeled values; missing facts remain unknown and keep the build or upsell blocked. A changed capture invalidates its review fingerprint. Case clearance facts use the same listing evidence and remain separate from supplier stock/price approval.

For exact Amazon bestseller ASINs, refresh technical attributes through Catalog Items API v2022-04-01 in batches of at most 20 when the Amazon SP-API Product Listing role is enabled. A denied API response is an external access gate: preserve last-good facts, throttle retries, expose the reason in DEV, and continue using exact retailer-page/title facts. Do not treat generic item/package dimensions as component fit measurements.

Flexible savings may share genuine procurement savings while preserving the segment's profit floors. Fast Track premiums must cover incremental costs and the configured contribution target. A global surcharge cannot establish viability for every segment. A market-supported price below the profitable floor means an unavailable offer or a reviewed build revision, never an automatic loss-making sale.

## Continuous commercial maintenance

Background processes refresh supplier price, stock, condition and delivery evidence; invalidate affected quotes; and reassess the core build and every upsell per mode. A failed upsell must not hide an otherwise viable core build. A failed Fast Track route must not hide a viable Standard or Flexible route. Missing, stale or contradictory mandatory evidence prevents purchase of the affected offer.

Use event-driven invalidation when observations change plus scheduled reconciliation. Jobs need durable run records, input/output versions, idempotency, bounded retries/timeouts, concurrency control, last-success/freshness indicators and alerts. Existing paid orders keep their accepted commercial snapshot; changed procurement evidence triggers a separate exception workflow.

Automatically refresh observations, expire unsafe quotes and withdraw offers that fail approved rules. Automatically publish reprices only within an explicitly enabled, bounded policy. Larger price movements, changed specifications/conditions, new paid services, segment retirement and broader promotion experiments produce reviewable proposals with evidence and rollback. Do not assign build slots; use evidenced workshop readiness/capacity and the existing delivery-priority model.

## Demand, promotions and learning

Combine exact-product Amazon bestseller rank history, sampled vendor completed/sold evidence, Google/search interest, and FlipFlop's own searches, configuration choices, quotes, abandoned checkouts, purchases and returns. Preserve source, observation period, geography, sample size, entity/cell mapping, freshness and confidence. A bestseller rank is not sales volume; sampled completed listings are not total market sales; search interest is not purchase intent.

Detect sustained changes against comparable historical windows, seasonality and adequate samples. Produce emerging-demand watchlists and proposals to add, reshape or retire builds and upsells. Upcoming trends are uncertain hypotheses supported by leading signals; do not describe them as known future demand. Missing sources lower confidence and cannot become zero demand.

Recommend promotion focus by segment and mode using expected incremental contribution, conversion evidence, delivery feasibility and risk. High demand alone does not justify a discount; low demand alone does not establish that a discount will help. Record the proposed audience, duration, budget, control/comparison, minimum margin, expiry and stop rules. Honour active accepted quotes according to their expiry and revalidate changed configurations before payment.

Learn from predicted versus realised procurement cost, delivery time, conversion, contribution, returns and warranty cost. Begin with observable rules and calibrated parameters; introduce more complex models only when data supports measurable improvement. Backtest/time-split evaluation and bounded experiments must beat the existing policy before promotion. LLMs may explain evidence and draft proposals; deterministic services own money, eligibility, compatibility and state changes.

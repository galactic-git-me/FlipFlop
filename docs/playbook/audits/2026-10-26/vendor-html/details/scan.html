1TB Kingston NV3 M.2 NVMe PCIe 4.0 SSD

For high Speed, Low Power Storage Needs. Kingston's NV3 PCIe 4.0 NVMe SSD is a next-gen storage solution powered by a Gen 4x4 NVMe controller by Phison, delivering read/write speeds of up to 6,000/4,000MB/s. It offers lower power consumption and reduced heat, optimising your system's performance without compromising value. The compact, single-sided M.2 2280 (22x80mm) design expands storage by 1TB, conserving space for other components. Experience NVMe speeds with NV3.

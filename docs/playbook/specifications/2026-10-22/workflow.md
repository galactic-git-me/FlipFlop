# Evolution workflow

Proposed process 0.2.1. One JSON specification source, generated readable matrix, linked immutable
evidence, append-only decision records and ordinary Git history. No database, scheduler or new service.

The capability-led dimension and the narrowed scope recorded in specification 0.2.1 are owner-approved:
withhold generic Advanced Student/Office/Family, share the overlapping gaming BOM, and redesign
Student Essential as a serviceable integrated-graphics desktop while retaining all 24 IDs.
Individual profile names, acceptance tests, exact BOMs and prices remain draft.
Keep `capability_tier` separate from `legacy_tier`:
the former defines customer benefit and rank; the latter preserves existing software, policy and order
associations until a separately reviewed runtime migration. Customer budget is a request constraint,
not a capability level. Derive the payable price after validating an appropriate complete build and
market position. Archived price experiments must never select a profile or manufacture demand.

Use schema version for contract changes, specification version for workload/capability/default changes,
supplier revision for exact product/offer refresh within approved constraints, commercial policy version
for allowances/fees/margin/VAT changes. A supplier refresh cannot alter architecture, memory type,
internal storage role, promised noise, licence rights or runtime support. An alternative outside the
approved boundary is a new specification proposal, even when it benchmarks faster.

Researcher drafts a branch and records evidence -> regenerate/validate -> reviewer checks exact-source
compatibility and workload results -> owner approves exact revision/hash and decisions in changes.jsonl
-> separately scoped implementation maps to existing cell IDs and existing approval/publication gates.
Approval of this document does not enable current bounded auto-publication. An LLM may propose, never
approve, buy, mutate live offers or publish. Paid order snapshots remain immutable.

Each change event has event_id, timestamp, actor, kind, affected_cell_ids, before_version, after_version,
reason, evidence_refs, decision, approver, commit and supersedes. Null approver/commit means unapproved
or not committed; do not invent an actor signature. Owner rejection is retained. Rollback uses a new
reviewed commit restoring a previous spec, with a new history event and fresh supplier/economics checks;
never restore expired stock/price approvals or rewrite order history.

Cadence: weekly manual supplier/economics review for candidate offers, monthly workload/demand triage,
quarterly specification review. Before every quote, refresh exact cost/stock/postage/licensing/fit evidence
through existing gates. Suggested research limits: supplier/active quotes 7 days, sold window 30 days
(90 days only as explicitly labelled historical context), core specification 90 days, software support
30 days. These draft limits do not override stricter live policy. Expiry means unknown, not zero demand.

Trigger an immediate proposal for a relevant product release (never automatically promote it), stock
loss or supplier withdrawal, >5% landed BOM movement or any floor breach, >10% competitive delivered
price change, new application/model/driver support, a failed acceptance test, material customer feedback,
a safety/fit issue or repeated return cause. One substantiated critical incident triggers review; do not
wait for a sample. Review conversion and returns descriptively below 10 orders; at >=10 report counts,
period and uncertainty, not causal uplift. A new cheaper part requires the same capability and fit tests.

Evidence records must retain raw observation location/hash, source item and physical-product identities,
capture/transaction dates, exact selected configuration, price basis, currency/region, VAT/delivery/fees,
condition, availability, hidden-offer flag, exclusions, reviewer and methodology version. Do not overwrite
raw captures when decisions change. Existing local snapshots are referenced with hashes in evidence.json;
missing archived web bytes are explicitly a reproducibility limitation. Preserve permitted captures in a
future approved collection run. Keep secrets and authenticated page contents out of committed artifacts.

Publishable economics require every selected item and supplier postage known, compatible assembled
system with firmware/clearance/power/connectors checked, licence rights and selected software confirmed,
workload acceptance passed, current active and sold evidence kept separate, both contribution floors
met and capability-specific customer outcomes substantiated. Among enabled comparable offers, investigate
price inversions and overlap against exact configurations, warranty and service; do not manufacture a
price ladder or reject a cheaper capable machine solely because of its rank. Capability progression is
a workload requirement, not a price ordering invariant. Current legacy publication rules remain unchanged
until separately migrated. Withheld/merged tiers must show their disposition. A higher-cost profile without
a useful validated outcome is redesigned or withheld; a lower-cost profile is preferred if it meets the need.

Schema 2.0.0 / spec 0.2.0 preserves 0.1.0 in `history/0.1.0.json` with its schema and evidence register;
the event records the archive hash. Validate an archived specification against its own archived schema.
Do not overwrite historical snapshots. Rollback of runtime offers still requires current evidence and
separate approval; restoring a specification never reinstates expired stock or price approvals.

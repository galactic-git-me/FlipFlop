## Interpretation and scope limits

**Student Hybrid, Business Office and Family Home:** R01–R05 support investigating integrated-graphics desktops before allocating money to dedicated GPUs. R02 supplies a useful £550 advertised complete desk bundle anchor, with substantial availability and configuration caveats. Student Hybrid gaming capability must still be defined and benchmarked; an iGPU cannot silently satisfy a demanding gaming requirement. R01's older four-core CPU and small SATA drive make it a price floor comparison, not an automatic recommended build. Business Pro licensing, display outputs, networking and support terms must be matched explicitly.

**Gaming:** R06/R07 show why a £900–£1,100 advertised machine must be normalised for OS, memory, storage, GPU VRAM and warranty before positioning FlipFlop. They do not establish a market median or a profitable FlipFlop selling price.

**Development/creation/AI:** This pass did not establish fresh, fully configured high-memory developer, creator or AI complete-system quotations. Do not stretch R06/R07 into evidence of suitability for large local models, CUDA workloads, professional video timelines or sustained compilation. Obtain exact workload/software requirements and then quote matched RAM, VRAM, SSD and warranty configurations. The RAM prices in R08/R09 are meaningful current evidence but do not establish a complete BOM or every retailer's price level.

No full candidate procurement BOM was priced in this pass. CPU, board, cooler, case, PSU, SSD, licence, freight, assembly, payment fees and warranty/returns reserves remain required for a landed commercial calculation. A cheaper component basket cannot be inferred from a competitor complete-system headline.

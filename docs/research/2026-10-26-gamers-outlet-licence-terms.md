# Gamers Outlet Windows licence source review

**Reviewed:** 26 October 2026  
**Purpose:** First-party source check for Playbook Windows 11 Home/Pro supplier evidence. This is not legal advice or an approval to resell/preinstall the keys.

## Findings

- Gamers Outlet's current Windows 11 Home listing is labelled **OEM**, **Global**, and **Lifetime License**. It describes a 25-character key, says it is tied to one device/motherboard, and says the key is activated on the user's installed Windows system. The page displayed a GBP price of £3.47 when checked; prices can change. This supports the identity and advertised product claims for the captured Home offer, but those are seller claims, not independent confirmation of Microsoft's licence channel. [Gamers Outlet: Windows 11 Home](https://www.gamers-outlet.net/en/buy-windows-11-home-cd-key-retail-microsoft-global?currency=GBP)
- The Windows 11 Pro listing likewise identifies an OEM global key, and describes reinstall/transfer only when linked to a Microsoft account. [Gamers Outlet: Windows 11 Pro OEM](https://www.gamers-outlet.net/en/buy-windows-11-pro-cd-key-oem-microsoft-global-1)
- Gamers Outlet's About page says its game keys are genuine keys scanned from physical products, describes keys as becoming the buyer's property after purchase, and directs bulk buyers to contact it for wholesale. This page does not provide a Windows system-builder agreement, per-device reseller permission, or permission to preinstall and transfer an OEM licence to FlipFlop customers. [Gamers Outlet: About Us](https://www.gamers-outlet.net/en/about-us)
- Its Terms say users must comply with applicable end-user licence agreements and prohibit public, business, or commercial use of the **Service**. The page does not clearly resolve whether a business may buy the listed consumer OEM key and install it on a PC it sells. The terms also disclaim product warranties to the extent allowed by law. This is ambiguity, not affirmative authorization. [Gamers Outlet: Terms & Conditions](https://www.gamers-outlet.net/en/terms-and-conditions)

## Decision for FlipFlop

The pages support recording the exact listing as **seller-described OEM, Global, in stock, digital key** for internal source review. They do **not** establish that FlipFlop may resell or preinstall the key with a customer PC, nor do they independently validate the licence's Microsoft entitlement. A low price, a seller's “OEM” label, and an advertised global activation claim are not reseller/system-builder authorization.

Therefore:

- Internal listing identity and price capture may be reviewed as supplier evidence.
- Do not mark this source as approved for customer licensing, system-builder preinstallation, or storefront sale on the public information found here.
- Keep all 24 Playbook cells blocked at the software-source gate until FlipFlop receives written, product-specific clarification from Gamers Outlet and verifies the applicable Microsoft OEM/system-builder terms. A wholesale contact invitation alone is not such confirmation.

## Source limits

The seller's Terms page returned HTTP 403 to direct page retrieval during review; the indexed first-party page text was available in the search provider. The product listing and About page were directly readable. This evidence can change; recheck pages and record the exact price and page content at the time a future supplier approval is considered.

# Indirect-channel build-portal access

**Reviewed:** 29 September 2026  
**Status:** Operational guidance; recheck before changing marketplace behavior

## Decision

For eBay and Vinted sales, deliver the private build portal claim route as a printed QR leaflet inside the parcel. Do not send the FlipFlop URL through marketplace chat, marketplace-mediated email, listings or profiles. Keep order and delivery updates on the marketplace where its rules require it. Dispatch Centre does not expose the raw marketplace claim URL.

For a direct guest purchase, use the verified checkout email only through the transactional email flow. The private leaflet is the recovery option if email delivery fails.

## Sources

- eBay's UK member-to-member policy says external website links and contact details are restricted in member communications; its spam and off-eBay transaction policies also limit use of buyer contact information. [Member-to-member contact policy](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/default/default/membertomember-contact-policy?id=4262), [Spam policy](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/policies/member-behavior-policies/spam-policy?id=5033), [Offering to buy or sell outside eBay](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/payment-policies/offers-buy-sell-outside-ebay-policy/offering-buy-sell-outside-ebay-policy?id=4272)
- Vinted's published community standards say to keep conversations on Vinted and not share personal or financial information. Its catalogue rules prohibit external-site references or links in listings and profiles. [Community standards](https://www.vinted.com/help/349-communityregler), [Catalog rules](https://www.vinted.com/catalog-rules)

These pages describe platform rules, not legal advice. Recheck the relevant UK seller account terms and channel workflow before using any exception. The buyer's leaflet is a post-sale product-access insert, not a request to transact outside the marketplace.

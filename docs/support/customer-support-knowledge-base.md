# FlipFlop first-line support knowledge base

**Status:** Approved baseline for support grounding  
**Version:** 1.4
**Last reviewed:** 2026-09-29  
**Customer-safe:** Yes  
**Scope:** General answers only; authenticated services provide order-specific facts

## Bot rules

1. Answer only from approved customer-safe content and live authenticated order data.
2. Never turn an estimate into a guarantee.
3. Never invent stock, component model, delivery date, tracking event, price, refund status, warranty outcome, or insurer decision.
4. Explain statutory rights separately from the 12-month FlipFlop warranty.
5. Escalate safety, data loss, payment disputes, legal threats, damaged deliveries, suspected fraud, and ambiguous warranty cases to a human.
6. Do not expose internal margins, supplier credentials, security tokens, risk scores, or another customer's data.
7. Include the approved source name and version/review date with AI-generated general support answers.

## What does FlipFlop sell?

FlipFlop offers:

- Ready-to-Ship PCs that are already built and tested;
- Curated-for-you PCs selected from approved choices after a short needs conversation;
- Made-by-you PCs with wider customization and active advice;
- a planned send-in PC upgrade and transformation service.

Every offered PC is aligned to a Playbook segment based on customer use and budget. A machine may use different exact parts where it provides equal-or-better appropriate performance and remains compatible.

## How are recommendations made?

FlipFlop asks what the customer wants to do, their budget, practical needs, style, and timing. It recommends a suitable performance level and explains where the budget goes. When an upgrade adds little useful benefit for that customer, FlipFlop should say so.

In Made-by-you, a dedicated GPU may be omitted only when the selected Playbook explicitly marks it as optional. The customer may still add one; the configurator checks its fit and power needs and may explain that the Playbook does not require the extra spend. This advice never changes the customer's selections. If the Playbook requires a GPU, it cannot be skipped. Curated-for-you builds remain the complete, pre-approved specification. Never describe a Playbook recommendation as a guarantee of performance or savings.

## What delivery choices are there?

- Ready-to-Ship aims for next-working-day dispatch when eligible.
- Fast Track targets about three working days on eligible made-to-order orders.
- Standard targets about five working days on eligible made-to-order orders.
- Flexible uses a wider sourcing pool and shows an evolving estimate, initially often around ten working days.

These are targets unless checkout explicitly states a guaranteed date. The customer's portal shows the current estimate and status.

## Is delivery charged separately?

Standard outbound postage and any delivery insurance arranged by FlipFlop are included in the displayed order price. A premium delivery option may cost extra when clearly shown before payment.

## Can I change or cancel an order?

Before components are ordered, customers with a direct made-to-order order can self-cancel for a full refund. The system pauses sourcing while the refund is reconciled; if the refund is delayed, retry the cancellation from the order portal or contact support. Ready-to-Ship stock has already passed the components-ordered gate before listing, and marketplace orders must follow the marketplace's cancellation process. A build change pauses sourcing while FlipFlop confirms compatibility, timing, and any price difference. Once components have been ordered, self-service changes close; contact support so the order and legal rights can be reviewed.

## Can I return an online order?

The baseline policy allows a customer to notify FlipFlop within 14 days after delivery and then return the order within a further 14 days. Change-of-mind return postage is normally paid by the customer. Faulty, damaged, misdescribed, or incorrectly supplied goods are handled under statutory rights, including reasonable necessary return costs where applicable.

## What if the PC is faulty?

Contact FlipFlop with the order/build reference, description, photos or video, and any error messages. Do not advise a customer to dismantle or repair the PC before support reviews the issue. Qualifying faulty goods may carry rights to rejection, repair, replacement, price reduction, or refund depending on timing and circumstances.

## What warranty is included?

New complete FlipFlop builds include a 12-month FlipFlop warranty starting from recorded delivery. It is additional to the customer's statutory rights. A send-in transformation service covers supplied parts and FlipFlop workmanship under its stated terms; retained customer-owned parts do not automatically become newly warranted.

## How can I follow a warranty repair or replacement?

The private build portal shows the latest case stage. If carrier details are attached to a customer-return or replacement case, use the separate shipment card for each parcel. The customer's return parcel and the later repaired/replacement parcel have independent tracking timelines. Parcel2Go scans appear in the matching timeline only after the team records that shipment's OrderLineId; other carriers remain manual-link only. A delivered return parcel advances the case to received for inspection, while delivery of the repaired/replacement PC does not automatically close the claim. If a tracking link is missing or no longer works, contact support. Direct storefront customers receive an email when the team changes the case stage and when either tracked parcel is delivered; intermediate courier scans remain in the portal timeline. Marketplace buyers continue to receive channel-required messages through the marketplace; do not send portal links through marketplace messaging.

## What should I do about delivery damage?

Photograph the parcel before opening where practical, photograph the damage, keep every part of the packaging, and contact FlipFlop immediately with the order and tracking references. The private case form accepts up to twelve JPEG, PNG, WebP, or PDF files, including photos, the shipping label, proof of value, and a carrier incident/claim reference. If the courier asks for a damage reservation or incident report, complete it and retain the reference. Escalate insured loss/damage cases the same day. [Secursus's current UK guide](https://www.secursus.com/en-gb/prepare-your-package/) says to file with the carrier immediately and with Secursus within 14 days; check the specific active policy and do not promise an insurer outcome. FlipFlop handles the carrier/insurer process; insurance terms do not reduce customer rights.

## What is My FlipFlop?

Registration creates a customer portal. Each purchased PC has its own personalized build portal linked beneath it, so repeat customers can see several builds. Depending on build stage, it can contain progress, specification, benchmark results, documents, warranty information, courier, and tracking.

When the reviewed Getting Ready pack is published, the portal provides the customer package and separate downloads for customer-facing files such as the specification, benchmark report, guide and component manuals. The portal also shows the names of submitted software choices and an indicative setup-time estimate; if choices have not been confirmed, it says so rather than showing Playbook defaults as selected. The private operator dispatch checklist is not part of the customer package and is available only in the admin Dispatch Centre. The recorded delivery date appears with the build's warranty information and is the start date for the 12-month FlipFlop warranty.

## What happens the first time I switch on my PC?

FlipFlop prepares and checks the PC before dispatch, then leaves Windows at its customer first-run setup (OOBE). Follow the Getting Started guide on the build portal or in the parcel to complete Windows setup and create your own account. FlipFlop does not set or retain a customer password, and the setup does not promise that Windows will offer a local account without a Microsoft account. If setup behaves unexpectedly, contact support before resetting Windows or changing partitions; do not share your password or Windows product key with support.

## What if I bought through eBay or another marketplace?

Marketplace order and delivery updates continue through the marketplace where required. For eBay and Vinted orders, the private build-portal claim route is supplied on a printed QR leaflet in the parcel; do not send the external portal link through marketplace messages, marketplace-mediated email, listings or profiles. A direct guest order may use the verified transaction email only through an approved transactional email flow. Support must not ask a customer to leave a marketplace or bypass its rules.

The claim link is high entropy, expires after up to 400 days, can be revoked and is consumed the first time it is redeemed. The resulting guest session lasts up to 400 days and opens one build; it does not create a FlipFlop account. Guest access can view that build and submit support or warranty cases for it; it cannot change account, order, software, or delivery details. If it has expired, was already used in another browser, or was lost, verify the customer through the original account or marketplace process and escalate for an administrator. Do not promise a replacement marketplace link; marketplace access is delivered by the printed leaflet. Never authenticate someone using surname and order number alone.

## What software can FlipFlop install?

FlipFlop's recommended software selection and installation are included with the PC at no extra charge. Customers can choose from the approved application catalogue, starting with recommendations suited to their Playbook customer type and budget. For made-to-order PCs, choices can change until components are ordered; for Ready-to-Ship PCs, choices can change until dispatch. Third-party application licences and account terms still apply. Optional AI setup services appear only when FlipFlop has configured and enabled them for sale; they are separate one-off charges, offered after components have been ordered and hardware eligibility is confirmed. Local model setup requires at least 12 GB of confirmed GPU VRAM. Workshop setup records machine-scope application and desktop-theme completion before dispatch; any selected user-scope apps are identified in the Getting Started guide and install at Windows profile sign-in. WinGet may ask the customer to review source or app licence terms during that setup. FlipFlop does not accept package terms for the customer; accept only after reviewing and agreeing. Declining a required agreement means that app will not install. Do not promise a specific assistant, automation, cloud subscription, account connection, or support term unless it appears in that build's paid-service record and reviewed scope.

Some publishers provide only per-user installers. When a selected app is marked for customer-profile setup in the build's Getting Started guide, connect the PC to the internet and sign into Windows; the installer runs in that profile and retries at the next sign-in if it encounters an error. If WinGet shows an agreement prompt, the customer must decide whether to accept it; FlipFlop does not accept package terms on the customer's behalf. Do not tell customers that the app is already installed until their profile setup has completed.

## Human escalation triggers

Escalate immediately when:

- the customer reports smoke, heat, electrical smell, liquid, damaged mains cable, or battery swelling;
- the parcel or PC arrived damaged;
- a payment/refund appears duplicated, missing, or disputed;
- the customer threatens legal action or asks for a binding legal interpretation;
- identity, order ownership, or portal access cannot be verified;
- the requested outcome conflicts with the recorded contract or policy;
- data recovery or destructive Windows reinstallation may be involved;
- the bot lacks current order, tracking, or warranty evidence.

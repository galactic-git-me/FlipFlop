# About FlipFlop

## How It Works

FlipFlop is an intelligent PC sourcing and reselling platform that helps you find, evaluate, and sell computer hardware profitably.

### The Three-Step Process

1. **Source Intelligence** - Our browser extension continuously monitors multiple marketplaces (eBay, Amazon, Vinted, Overclockers, and more) for new listings. Each listing is automatically evaluated against real market data for value and demand potential.

2. **Smart Scoring** - Every listing receives a deal score (0-10) based on:
   - Real sold-price comparables (not asking prices)
   - Component compatibility and current-generation status
   - Market confidence and buyer demand signals
   - Risk factors and price misalignment detection

3. **Actionable Recommendations** - Listings are classified into clear decision categories:
   - **SUPER GEM** - Exceptional deals worth buying immediately
   - **GEM** - Strong value opportunities
   - **OK DEAL** - Fair value buys
   - **POOR DEAL** - Priced above market, pass

### Key Features

**Real Benchmark Data** - Performance cards attached to builds show actual frame rates and benchmark scores from real systems, not marketing specs. Buyers see exactly what they're getting.

**Trust Through Honesty** - Our selling framework emphasizes factual specifications, actual condition notes, and realistic pricing. This builds buyer confidence and results in faster sales and better prices.

**Market Intelligence** - Watch trends across vendors, track pricing patterns, and understand which components and configurations command premium resale values in your market.

**Opportunity Discovery** - Our algorithms identify underpriced listings and market gaps before they're widely noticed. Early discovery means lower acquisition costs and faster inventory turns.

## Why FlipFlop Works

- **Speed** - Automated evaluation means you see opportunities in real-time, not days later
- **Data** - Pricing is anchored to real sold comps, not asking prices, so valuations are grounded in reality
- **Focus** - Only actionable opportunities surface; noise is filtered out
- **Scale** - Monitor thousands of listings across multiple marketplaces simultaneously

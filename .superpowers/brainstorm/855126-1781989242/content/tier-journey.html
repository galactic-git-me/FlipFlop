Where does Budget / Mid / High End appear?

Three different customer journeys — pick the one that feels right for your site.

A

Tier first, then playbook

Customer starts by setting their budget, then picks a use case.

Budget £300-500 → Gaming Rig → Configure

Works well for price-sensitive buyers who know their budget before their use case.

B

Playbook first, then tier

Customer picks their use case, then chooses how much to spend.

Gaming Rig → Mid-Range £600-900 → Configure

Natural — most people know what they want to use it for before they know what to spend.

C

Tier as a starting preset, not a gate

Customer picks playbook, arrives at configurator with 3 pre-built starting points to choose from or customise.

Gaming Rig → Pick a starting point → Tweak it
Starter
from £349
Battle-Ready
from £649
Beast Mode
from £999

Most flexible — customers can start cheap and upgrade components, or start premium and cut back. Tier names can be playbook-specific (Starter / Battle-Ready / Beast Mode for gaming).

How tiers work under the hood

Tier = a gem score band + price ceiling. Budget = gems scoring 40-65, Mid = 65-80, High = 80+. Your existing gem scoring system already provides this — tiers are just filters on top of it. No extra data entry.

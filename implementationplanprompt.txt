Process the automation roadmap in profit-impact order, highest first. For
each row (and each existing component found in step 0):
1. Restate what it does in 1-2 sentences, using the row's "Why it moves your

2. Decide and state explicitly where it belongs:

4. Where a genuine open decision remains (see above), propose the default

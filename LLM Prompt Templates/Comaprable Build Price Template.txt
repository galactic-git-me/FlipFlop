Research requirements:

Search multiple reputable UK retailers, marketplaces, system builders, and specialist suppliers.
Prioritise complete builds that exactly match the specification.
If there are not enough exact matches, include the closest comparable builds, but clearly explain every difference, such as component model, capacity, performance tier, condition, warranty, or included accessories.
Only include listings that are currently in stock and available to buy in the UK. Do not use old search snippets, cached prices, expired deals, auction bids, or unavailable products.
Use new products unless the specification explicitly requests used or refurbished items.
Record prices in GBP and include VAT. Include mandatory delivery charges in the total where known. Show delivery separately.
Avoid duplicate listings. Treat different sellers offering the same product as separate observations only when each listing is genuine and independently purchasable.
Open and verify each product page rather than relying solely on search-result text.
Record the date and time when the prices were checked.

Price analysis:

Create one calculation for exact matches if at least three valid exact matches are available.
Create a separate calculation for close comparable matches if they are needed.
Identify suspicious price anomalies using a defensible statistical method, preferably the 1.5×IQR rule. If the sample is too small for that method, assess anomalies cautiously using the median and the listings’ specification differences.
Do not remove a price merely because it is the highest or lowest.
Exclude a listing only when there is a clear reason, such as an obvious pricing error, materially different specification, missing VAT, incomplete build, scam-like listing, auction price, used condition, or an extreme statistical outlier.
Show every excluded listing separately, including its price and the exact reason for exclusion.
Never silently discard a result.

Accuracy rules:

Do not invent prices, listings, specifications, stock information, or links.
Cite every included and excluded listing with a direct product-page link.
Clearly distinguish verified facts from assumptions.
If live web access is unavailable, stop and say so rather than estimating.
